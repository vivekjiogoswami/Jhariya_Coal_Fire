Research & Evidence

Reading Jharia through evidence

Spatial digitization workflow

  1. 01 · Scan & inspect. Identify British-era and post-nationalization mine survey maps, map marginalia, legends and coordinate references.
  2. 02 · Georeference in QGIS. Select stable ground-control points, assign CRS, transform, inspect residuals and document uncertainty.
  3. 03 · Digitize. Trace colliery boundaries, mine-fire areas, roads, settlements and relocation sites into GeoJSON/PostGIS.
  4. 04 · Remote sensing. Compare Landsat thermal infrared signals with Sentinel-1 InSAR displacement/coherence products across decades.
  5. 05 · Provenance. Store source, processing date, operator, CRS, resolution and transformation notes alongside each layer.

Internet data extraction pipeline

Harvest → validate → contextualize → archive

A research workflow for discoverable internet material, with human validation and provenance at every stage.

01

Discovery

USGS EarthExplorer, ESA Copernicus Hub, GEE, government portals, legal repositories, Wayback, YouTube.

02

Harvest

Metadata APIs, permitted downloads, archived URLs, document capture, audio acquisition and checksums.

03

Enrich

Whisper STT, OCR, named entities, dialect labels, geocoding and temporal normalization.

04

Validate

Manual dialect validation, source comparison, consent checks, uncertainty flags and human review.

05

Archive

PostGIS, object storage, Dublin Core, WMS/WFS and archival CMS with provenance.

Research ethics & provenance

Archive with accountability

Oral histories require informed consent, culturally appropriate access controls, anonymization where requested, and careful representation of dialect. Remote-sensing and legal materials should retain source URLs, acquisition dates, licenses, processing steps, and uncertainty statements.
